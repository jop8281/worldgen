A dog-walking service. Owners register their dogs and book walks with a walker for a time slot, then pay after the walk. A walker takes at most 4 dogs on one walk and cannot be booked into two overlapping slots. A booking can be cancelled for free until 2 hours before the slot; a later cancellation is charged half the price. Walkers mark a walk started and completed, and a walk not started 30 minutes after its slot begins is flagged as missed.
