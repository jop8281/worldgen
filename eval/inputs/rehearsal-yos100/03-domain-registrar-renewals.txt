Something like Namecheap: customers own domains registered for 1 to 10 years, and each domain has an expiry date, an auto-renew flag and a wallet balance that pays for renewals. Reminders go out 30 days and 7 days before expiry; on the expiry date auto-renew charges the wallet if it covers the price, otherwise the domain enters a 30-day grace period, then a 30-day redemption period at a higher fee, and is then released. Agents renew domains (including ones in grace or redemption), turn auto-renew on or off, top up wallets, and move a domain between customers.
