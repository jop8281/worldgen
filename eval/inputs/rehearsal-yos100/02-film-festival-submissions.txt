Something like FilmFreeway for one film festival. Filmmakers submit films to categories (short, feature, documentary), each with a runtime and a fee that can be waived with a waiver code. A submission moves from received to screening to shortlisted, then to selected or declined, and it can't be shortlisted until at least two judges have scored it from 1 to 10. Agents assign judges, record scores, apply fee waivers, and notify filmmakers of decisions; a declined film can't be selected without being re-screened.
