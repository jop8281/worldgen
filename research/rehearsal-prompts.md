# Rehearsal prompts: unseen bank

These are the prompts for the live-run rehearsal (plan.md §9, "Rehearsal protocol"). YOS-58 and YOS-51 read this file.

There are 20 one-line descriptions: 13 normal, 5 vague and 2 impossible. They avoid the domains in the stress suite (`eval/suite.yaml`) so that they stay unseen. Run each prompt exactly as written, with the default config. If a prompt fails, fix the generic cause (hints, stage prompts), never the specific prompt. These are description-only prompts. The protocol's OpenAPI or CSV case comes from somewhere else.

Columns: **domain**, **kind** (normal / vague / impossible), and **good result**, a one-sentence description of what a passing WorldGen run looks like.

| # | Prompt | Domain | Kind | Good result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | An expense management tool where employees submit card transactions with receipts, managers approve them, and finance exports approved batches to the ledger. | fintech | normal | A transaction workflow (pending → submitted → approved/rejected → exported) whose guards block export of unapproved items and items above a threshold with no receipt, plus a task that requires approving before exporting. |
| 2 | A peer-to-peer payments app with wallets, transfers between users, daily send limits, and pending transfers the sender can cancel. | fintech | normal | Balances in minor units. A transfer debits and credits atomically, a daily limit refuses an over-limit transfer with no balance change, and a cancelled pending transfer restores the sender's balance. |
| 3 | A last-mile delivery dispatcher: drivers, routes, parcels with delivery windows, failed delivery attempts, and return-to-depot after three failed attempts. | logistics | normal | A parcel workflow in which the third failed attempt moves the parcel to returning. Seed data has drivers carrying a believable mix of parcel states, and a task makes the agent reassign a route without breaking delivery windows. |
| 4 | Freight booking for a trucking broker: shippers post loads, carriers bid, the broker awards each load to one carrier, and the shipment moves through pickup, in transit and delivered with proof of delivery. | logistics | normal | Only one bid per load can be awarded. Delivered requires a proof-of-delivery record, and the graders check the awarded carrier and the final state, not the order of calls. |
| 5 | An applicant tracking system with job requisitions, candidates, interview stages, scorecards, and offers that need approval from both the hiring manager and HR. | HR | normal | An offer workflow that stays pending until it has both approvals, recorded as separate actors. A candidate cannot skip interview stages, and a hard task makes the agent find the strongest-scored candidate across scorecards before extending an offer. |
| 6 | Payroll run management: salaried and hourly employees, timesheets, a payroll run that locks the timesheets it covers, and corrections through off-cycle runs. | HR | normal | Once a run is finalized, edits to its timesheets are refused, so corrections go through an off-cycle run. Pay amounts in seed data are consistent with hours and rates. |
| 7 | Operating-room scheduling for a hospital: surgeons, rooms, procedures with durations, no overlapping cases, a cleaning buffer between cases, and emergency cases that bump elective ones. | healthcare scheduling | normal | Overlap and buffer rules are enforced on writes, using the engine clock. An emergency booking moves the bumped elective case to needs-rescheduling instead of silently deleting it. |
| 8 | A home-care nurse visit scheduler where visits need a nurse with matching certifications, patients have preferred time windows, and a missed visit must be rescheduled within 48 hours. | healthcare scheduling | normal | Assigning a nurse who lacks a certification is refused. A missed visit has a 48-hour deadline measured on the virtual clock, and a task makes the agent find a qualified nurse who is free in the patient's window. |
| 9 | A CI service: repositories, pipelines, jobs with dependencies, runners, and retries for flaky jobs, where a pipeline passes only if all its required jobs pass. | devtools | normal | A job cannot start before its dependencies succeed, retries are capped, and a pipeline's status is derived from its required jobs. Graders tell "retried and passed" apart from "marked passed". |
| 10 | A feature flag service with environments, flags, percentage rollouts, targeting rules, and an audit log of every change. | devtools | normal | Rollout percentages are validated to 0–100 per environment, and every write appends an audit entry. A task makes the agent ramp a flag in staging only and leave production untouched. |
| 11 | University course registration with prerequisites, section capacity, waitlists, and add/drop deadlines. | education | normal | Registration is refused when a prerequisite is missing. A full section puts the student on the waitlist, a drop promotes the next waitlisted student, and add/drop deadlines are checked against the engine clock. |
| 12 | A restaurant reservation system with tables, party sizes, walk-ins, no-show tracking, and deposits for large parties. | hospitality | normal | Table capacity and time-slot conflicts are enforced, parties above a size threshold need a deposit before they are confirmed, and a no-show is recorded against the guest. |
| 13 | Building permit applications: submissions, plan review, inspections that can fail and be rescheduled, and permit issuance once every inspection has passed. | government | normal | A permit cannot be issued while any inspection is failed or pending. A failed inspection can be rescheduled, and the seed data has applications spread across every stage. |
| 14 | Something for managing our trucks. | logistics | vague | Picks a concrete analog (for example fleet maintenance: vehicles, service schedules, inspections, repair orders), names it in the plan, and lists each choice in `assumptions[]` and `open_questions[]` with defaults. It still builds a full, graded world. |
| 15 | An HR tool. | HR | vague | Commits to one named HR analog (for example onboarding checklists), not a grab-bag of everything HR. It records why it chose that analog, puts the other HR areas in `out_of_scope[]`, and builds a world that reads and writes. |
| 16 | Like Venmo but for teams. | fintech | vague | Reads "for teams" explicitly (for example shared team wallets with split requests) and writes that reading down as an assumption. Money stays in minor units and the world contains a real workflow, not just CRUD. |
| 17 | A dashboard for developers. | devtools | vague | Notices that a dashboard on its own has nothing to write. It picks a writable system underneath (for example deploys and incidents), records that reframe as its first assumption, and adds the read-only aggregate views on top. |
| 18 | An app for a dentist. | healthcare scheduling | vague | Picks appointments, patients and treatment plans, with assumptions on chair count, appointment lengths and the cancellation policy. Assumptions are written down, not silently guessed, and the report lists what was left out (insurance claims, imaging). |
| 19 | A world that predicts tomorrow's stock prices. | fintech | impossible | Ends in `.FAILED/` with a `FAILURE.md` saying that forecast accuracy is not world state a deterministic engine can serve or grade, and suggests a narrower request such as a paper-trading brokerage. It never ships a world that claims to predict. If it reframes instead of failing, the reframe must be the first assumption and prediction must be listed in `out_of_scope[]`. |
| 20 | A live air-traffic control system that ingests real ADS-B radar feeds and issues clearances to aircraft in flight. | aviation | impossible | Ends in `.FAILED/` with a `FAILURE.md` naming the blockers: a live external data feed, and real-time wall-clock behaviour that conflicts with the engine's virtual clock and fixed seed. It suggests a narrower request, such as a flight-strip and clearance tracker over seeded flights, and exits non-zero. |
