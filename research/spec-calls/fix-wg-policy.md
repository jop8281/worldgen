# Spec calls: fix-wg-policy

- `ownerOf` maps a `plan.not_covered` gap through its plan section to the stage that builds it: `plan/entities` and `plan/routes` -> model, `plan/workflows` (actions) -> workflow, `plan/tasks` -> tasks. Every other issue at `path[0] = plan` still maps to plan. Why: the plan already names the item, so the stage that left it out must fix it (A-13 ownership follows SECTION_OWNER). Reversible: yes.
- A gap found at a later step for an earlier stage's item (an entity missing at tasks) backtracks to that stage (model), not to plan. Why: same ownership rule as every other upstream error. Reversible: yes.
- `recordBacktrack(ledger, to)` takes the target and sets `attempts[to]` to 0. Seen issue sets are kept. Why: the target needs its full repair budget; an issue set that already repeated on that step is still no progress. `maxBacktracks` keeps the total bounded. Reversible: yes.
- An accepted outcome at or over `maxCostUsd` advances only on the run's last step (`LoopState.last`, default `step === 'tasks'`), with `overspent: { spentUsd, limitUsd }` on the decision. On any other step it stops `budget_exhausted`. Why: the budget forbids further calls, and a non-last step needs another call before a world exists. Reversible: yes.
- The time limit still stops an accepted last step. Why: not in the work order; kept as is. Reversible: yes.
- Conflict: red-team WG-P02 pins "accepted at or over the limit stops" on workflow and seed. The last-step rule satisfies both it and Acceptance 3, so no test changed.
- Follow-up: `RunEvent` (events.ts) has no warning variant. run.ts must emit the decision's `overspent` as a warning event once one exists (out of this unit's scope).
- (repair round 1) The `plan.not_covered` remap through the plan section applies only when `path[0] = plan`. A gap rooted at a world section (`seed/<entity>`, `routes/<op>`, `meta/api/error`, as wg-input-conformance emits) is owned by `path[0]` like any other `at_path` issue, so `seed/tasks` maps to seed, not tasks. Section lookups are own-key (`Object.hasOwn`), and a root with no owner (e.g. `constructor`) maps to plan, so `ownerOf` always returns a StepId. Why: Acceptance 1 scopes the remap to `path[0] = plan`; a prototype key must not leak `undefined` into `decide`. Reversible: yes.
