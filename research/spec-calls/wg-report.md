# Spec calls: wg-report

- `plan` is optional in `ReportInput`, unlike the backlog signature. Why: wg-repair-full writes REPORT.md on every exit, including a stop before a plan exists (an input rejected at digest, a model error on the plan step). Without a plan, Assumed and why and Left out say "No plan was made." Reversible: yes.
- On a stop, What was built and Proof claim nothing, even when a check report is passed. Changes, if a delta is passed, says none of them were written. Why: A-39 and A-40. A world the run did not hand over must not look built or proven. Reversible: yes.
- A stop report has no H1. Its first line is `Stopped: <kind>`, then the reason sentence, `No world.yaml was written.`, and the last issues. A done report opens with `# WorldGen report: <software>` and the plan summary. Why: acceptance 3 says the report "opens with" the stop line, and a check such as `startsWith('Stopped:')` should hold. Reversible: yes.
- Last issues come from the stop reason when it carries them (`attempts_exhausted`, `no_progress`). For the other kinds they come from the last `attempt` event when that attempt was rejected or invalid, and are "none recorded" when it was accepted or a model error. At most 10 are listed, and the rest are counted ("N more issues are in events.jsonl"). Why: acceptance 3 asks for the last issues on every stop, and a long list is no longer a short report. Reversible: yes.
- Run section: attempts, minutes and dollars per step are summed from `attempt` events, not `step_finished`, in first-seen order. A step that was started but never attempted shows 0. A step that stopped has no `step_finished`, so attempts are the only source that covers every step. `run_finished` total, when present, is printed on its own line ("Run total"), because it includes time spent outside attempts. Why: acceptance 1 (per step, from events), rubric H (cost and minutes equal events.jsonl). Reversible: yes.
- The Run section also shows the mode, model and budget from `run_started`, skipped steps with their why, and each backtrack with its issue count. `advice` events are never rendered. Why: out of scope says no model-written prose, and A-35 says commentary goes only to `advice`. Reversible: yes.
- Proof is a Markdown table, one row per `TaskVerdict` in `report.verdicts` order: task, difficulty, solution and noop (`toFixed(3)` of the verdict fields), decoy scores (`none` when empty), and best prefix (`n/a` when null). Decoy reasons are listed under the table. A lead line gives the check facts: world test count and warning count. Why: acceptance 1 says verdicts only, and spec says "proof checks and graders pass". Reversible: yes.
- What was built lists entities with `stats.rows`, routes and actions with method and path, and jobs with `every`, all in world key order. Tasks appear only in Proof, and state mixes are not listed. Why: acceptance 1 lists these items only. Reversible: yes.
- Changes: one line per `WorldChange` with its kind and the dotted path, plus ` (destructive)` when the kind is in the engine's `DESTRUCTIVE`. `before` and `after` values are not printed. Why: acceptance 2 asks for one line each, and snippet values span many lines. Reversible: yes.
- Model text (assumptions, out of scope items, decoy reasons, hints) is printed verbatim. Continuation lines are indented so they stay inside their list item. The title is collapsed to one line. Why: wg-repair-full acceptance 7 requires assumptions verbatim. Reversible: yes.
- `plan.open_questions` is not rendered. Why: answered questions are already recorded in `assumptions` (planSchemaFor), and acceptance 1 does not list them. Reversible: yes.
- The Proof test pins decoy scores, best prefix score, test count and warnings by spreading a real engine `TaskVerdict` and `CheckReport`, with no cast. engine-verify-full and engine-lints have not landed, so a real report has no decoy scores yet. Pinning keeps the literal text stable when they land. Why: the testing rules call for literal expected values. Reversible: yes.
